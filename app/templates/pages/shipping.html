Where we ship

Locoganga currently ships to addresses in the United States only.

Processing and delivery

  • Orders are processed within 1–3 business days.
  • Delivery typically takes 5–10 business days after processing, depending on the item and your location.
  • You will receive a confirmation email when your order is placed and a follow-up when it ships.

Stock availability

Our inventory is held by fulfillment partners and changes frequently. If an item becomes unavailable after you order, we will notify you by email and refund your payment in full.

Shipping costs and taxes

Standard shipping is free on orders of $35 or more. Orders under $35 ship for a flat $5.99. Your shipping cost is shown in your cart and again before you complete payment.
